How to Play

Objective: Work together to carry the bomb to the green extraction zone.

Up Key [W]: Snap vertical and thrust upwards immediately.

Down Key [S]: Automatically rotate retrograde (opposite to travel direction) and fire thrusters to brake.

Gamepad: Left Stick to Rotate, [A] Button to Thrust, Face Button [B] to Brake, bumper to clamp.

Harmony: Match flight angles to keep the bomb stable. Poor harmony or impacts cause stability loss.

Single Player: Toggle in Options. Use [Tab] or the on-screen button to switch ships. When both clamp the bomb, your controls pilot both simultaneously.
